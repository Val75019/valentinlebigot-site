About

Valentin Lebigot Art director & graphic designer.

Based in Paris, I help agencies, studios and brands design identities that come to life in space.

At the crossroads of branding, graphic design and spatial design, I create visual, wayfinding and scenographic systems and bespoke elements that extend brand identities beyond traditional media.

I mainly work on office, point-of-sale, retail, hospitality and place-identity projects, in collaboration with Design & Build agencies, creative studios and brands.
